# BLE device classes — what slothweb's waterfall sorts advertisers by

What the BLE Devices view's "who is around" panel (`web/src/web_bledev.c`
`web_ble_classes[]`, 2026-11-02) rests on. A class is a display grouping,
not a detector: no alert fires from it. It is still held to the owner's
standard for payload shapes (#145): a rule ships only when a source read
first-hand says what the field means, and a shape no source settles is
left out and named here. Every rule reads a field sloth's `ble_device`
record already carries (`mfr_id` and the first manufacturer octet
`mfr_subtype`, `uuid16`, `svc_data_uuid16`, `appearance`, `hid`,
`watch_category`); none needs a payload byte the stream does not have.

Read and quoted on 2026-11-02 unless the line says otherwise.

## Apple Continuity types (company 0x004C, the first octet)

Celosia and Cunche 2020, Table 3, "Summary of Apple Continuity messages
(CM) with their conditions of emission": AirPrint `0x03` on user
action, AirDrop `0x05` on user action, HomeKit `0x06` constantly,
Proximity Pairing `0x07` constantly, "Hey Siri" `0x08` on user action,
AirPlay `0x09` on user action, Magic Switch `0x0B` on user physical
action, Handoff `0x0C` on user action, Instant Hotspot Tethering Target
Presence `0x0D` on user action and Tethering Source Presence `0x0E` in
reaction to the target, Nearby Action `0x0F` on user action and Nearby
Info `0x10` constantly.

The same paper, §3.3, on who sends them:

- Proximity Pairing: "a feature to facilitate the pairing of audio
  devices with an iPhone or an iPad ... Proximity Pairing messages are
  constantly transmitted by active Apple audio devices (earphones and
  headphones)."
- HomeKit: "HomeKit messages are constantly transmitted by powered
  HomeKit devices".
- Magic Switch: "As observed by [1], only Watches send those messages."
- Nearby Info: "Nearby Info messages are continuously transmitted by
  iPhones, iPads, MacBooks and Watches regardless of the device status";
  §3.2, "advertisement packets generated by an iPhone will always
  include a Nearby Info message".
- Tethering Source Presence: "transmitted by devices capable of sharing
  a cellular connection".

Martin et al. 2019, Table 2 ("Most Commonly Observed Continuity
Messages"): Watch Connection 11, Handoff 12, Wi-Fi Settings 13, Instant
Hotspot 14, Wi-Fi Join Network 15, Nearby 16; §4.1.5, "An Apple Watch
transmits message type 11 ... This message distinctly identifies Apple
Watches as they are solely transmitted from an Apple Watch"; §4.1.4,
"AirPods transmit a unique message type".

The spam tool's `protocols/continuity.h` (ble-spam-flood.md) names the
same numbers: ProximityPair 0x07, NearbyAction 0x0F, AirDrop 0x05,
AirplayTarget 0x09, Handoff 0x0C, TetheringSource 0x0E, NearbyInfo 0x10.

So: 0x07 and 0x0B are **Wearables & audio**; 0x05, 0x0C, 0x0D, 0x0E,
0x0F and 0x10 are **Phones & computers** (Nearby Info is also sent by a
Watch: one that carries no Appearance lands with the phones, and the
page says so); 0x06 is **Vehicles & IoT**. Left out: 0x03 AirPrint and
0x08 "Hey Siri" (the sender is the device asking, of any kind), 0x09
AirPlay (the paper's message is sent by the device starting a stream,
the tool's name is the target's: the two disagree, so neither is
taken).

sloth keeps the **first** company and octet heard on a row
(`src/ble_hci.c`), and Apple concatenates several messages in one
advertisement (Martin et al. §4.1.3, "multiple Continuity message types
are often concatenated together in this TLV format"), so the class is
the first message's.

## Eddystone

google/eddystone `protocol-specification.md`: "Every Eddystone frame
type must contain the following PDU data types: The Complete List of
16-bit Service UUIDs ... must contain the Eddystone Service UUID of
`0xFEAA`" and "The Service Data - 16 bit UUID data type must be the
Eddystone Service UUID of `0xFEAA`." Either field equal to 0xFEAA is
**Beacons**.

## AltBeacon (left out)

AltBeacon/spec `README.md`: the Manufacturer Specific data is "MFG ID"
(any company's code, "the little endian representation of the beacon
device manufacturer's company code") then "BEACON CODE", "the big
endian representation of the value `0xBEAC`", two octets. The stream's
`mfr_subtype` is the first octet after the company id only, 0xBE, which
any company's data can start with, and the company is not fixed, so the
record cannot tell an AltBeacon. Not a rule.

## iBeacon (left out)

Apple's Proximity Beacon Specification was not read; the iBeacon type
octet (0x02 under 0x004C) is in neither paper's tables above (Martin
et al. §4.1.4 mentions iBeacon messages and does not analyse them). Not
a rule until a primary text is read.

## Appearance (SIG Assigned Numbers §2.6.2)

The category is the value's bits 15..6 (`web/data/btsig/appearance_values.csv`,
fetched 2026-10-27 per its `SOURCE.md`). Category 8 Tag and 9 Keyring
are **Trackers**; 1 Phone and 2 Computer **Phones & computers**; the
worn, audio and personal-health categories (3 Watch, 7 Eye-glasses, 13
Heart Rate Sensor, 14 Blood Pressure, 16 Glucose Meter, 17 Running
Walking Sensor, 18 Cycling, 33 Audio Sink, 34 Audio Source, 37 Wearable
Audio Device, 41 Hearing aid, 49 Pulse Oximeter, 52 Continuous Glucose
Monitor, 53 Insulin Pump, 54 Medication Delivery, 55 Spirometer, 81
Outdoor Sports Activity) **Wearables & audio**; the fixed, home,
vehicle and industrial ones (4 Clock, 5 Display, 6 Remote Control, 10
Media Player, 11 Barcode Scanner, 12 Thermometer, 19 Control Device to
32 Window Covering, 35 Motorized Vehicle, 36 Domestic Appliance, 38
Aircraft, 39 AV Equipment, 40 Display Equipment, 42 Gaming, 43 Signage,
50 Weight Scale, 51 Personal Mobility Device, 82 Industrial Measurement
Device, 83 Industrial Tools, 84 Cookware Device) **Vehicles & IoT**.
Category 15 (Human Interface Device) is not used directly: sloth's own
HID bits (0x03C0..0x03CA, or the HID service 0x1812 listed) are
**Input**, and they come before every Appearance rule. An Appearance is
what the advertiser says it is, not a verification.

## Tile and CHIPOLO (the registry's assignment)

ble-watchlist-categories.md, from `member_uuids.yaml` (read
2026-10-21): Tile, Inc. 0xFEED, 0xFEEC, 0xFD84; CHIPOLO d.o.o. 0xFE65,
0xFE33. Both companies make finder tags, so a UUID they were assigned,
listed or carried as Service Data, is **Trackers** here. That page's
caution holds and the page repeats it: the registry says who was
assigned a UUID, not what the payload means or that the advertiser is a
tag; this is a grouping on the assignment, which is why it is a class
and not a watchlist row.

## Apple Find My and Samsung SmartTag (left out)

ble-watchlist-categories.md lists both shapes (0x004C first octet 0x12;
Service Data 0xFD59 / 0xFD5A) as unverified. Not rules here either; the
Trackers row's footnote says so, so an empty row does not read as "no
trackers here".

## Microsoft, Google, Samsung

- [MS-CDP] 2.2.2.2.3, as read in ble-address-rotation.md: company
  0x0006, Scenario_Type 1, "Set to (1) Bluetooth scenario", the
  Connected Devices Platform beacon. Martin et al. §4.1.1: "only two
  major ecosystems are commonly observed utilizing BLE MAC address
  randomization – Apple and Microsoft Windows devices", the Microsoft
  ones by "Microsoft's Connected Devices Platform (MS-CDP) discovery
  protocol". 0x0006 first
  octet 0x01 is **Phones & computers**. Swift Pair (0x0006 first octet
  0x03, the peripheral's pairing beacon, ble-spam-flood.md) is not: the
  peripheral is a keyboard, a mouse or headphones, which its Appearance
  says when it carries one.
- Fast Pair "Provider Advertising signal", as read in
  ble-address-rotation.md: the Provider is the accessory being paired,
  advertising Service Data 0xFE2C (the 24-bit Model ID when
  discoverable, the account data otherwise). Service Data 0xFE2C is
  **Wearables & audio**; a listed 0xFE2C alone is not taken.
- Samsung 0x0075 first octet 0x42 (the buds packet) and 0x01 (the watch
  packet), as the BLE Spam tool's `easysetup.c` writes them
  (ble-spam-flood.md): **Wearables & audio**. The tool's comments are
  what was read; Samsung publishes no layout.

## The order

First match wins, in this order: Trackers, Input, Beacons, Wearables &
audio, Phones & computers, Vehicles & IoT, Unknown. A tracker that also
claims HID, or a beacon that also carries Continuity, is the earlier
class: the rarer and more specific claim is the one an operator scans
the waterfall for. `web/tests/test_bledev.c` holds each rule, each
overlap and each left-out shape.

## What it does not say

- A class names a kind of advertisement, never a device or a person.
- A rotating random address is its own row; nothing here links two.
- A class is decided per record from the fields sloth keeps, and a row
  whose ADV_IND and SCAN_RSP carry fields of two classes can be counted
  in each in turn.
