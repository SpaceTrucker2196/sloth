# T1595 — Active Scanning

Reconnaissance that touches the target. Everything here is an adversary
finding out what exists before deciding what to do about it, which is
why these alerts are early rather than urgent.

## Why sloth cites this

Seven alerts, spanning three layers and one that is not a layer at all:

**`PORT_SCAN`** (T1046, Network Service Discovery) — connection
attempts across many ports from one source.

**`PROBE_FLOOD`** — the wireless equivalent: probe requests at a rate a
client's own scanning does not reach.

**`ATTACK_TOOL_UA`** — a scanner naming itself in an HTTP User-Agent.
Cheap, high-confidence, and trivially evaded, which is a fair summary of
its value: it catches the unskilled and the unbothered.

**`ATTACK_PATH`** (T1190) — a request for a path that only exists in an
exploit list. Nobody browses to `/wp-admin/setup-config.php` on a host
that runs no WordPress.

**`RRM_SURVEY_ABUSE`** (T1595.002) — the interesting one. An 802.11k
Beacon Request makes *someone else's client* survey the air and report
back. The adversary's own radio never transmits a probe; the recon is
performed by a station that trusts its AP.

**`MY_NETWORK_RECON`** and **`RECURRING_TRANSIT`** are sloth's own
framing rather than a protocol observable — scanning aimed at the
operator's own network, and a device seen repeatedly over separate
visits. The second is physical reconnaissance: the same radio passing
the same place, which is a pattern only a persistent sensor can see.

## What it does not say

**Scanning is background radiation on the public internet.** A port
scan from an arbitrary source is not a finding; it is the weather. What
makes `MY_NETWORK_RECON` worth a different severity is the target, not
the technique.

**A User-Agent is a self-report.** `ATTACK_TOOL_UA` catches a tool that
did not bother to change its default. It says nothing about a tool that
did, and its absence is not evidence.

**`RECURRING_TRANSIT` needs care.** A device seen on three separate days
is a neighbour, a delivery driver, or a colleague far more often than it
is surveillance. Sloth reports the pattern; the operator supplies the
context, and MAC randomisation means even the pattern is only as good as
the correlation behind it.
