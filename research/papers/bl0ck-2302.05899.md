# Bl0ck — Block-Ack window paralysis

A spoofed Block-Ack Request sent on behalf of a legitimate station
forces its peer to advance the Block-Ack receive window past frames that
are still queued. Everything in the skipped range is discarded and the
connection stalls.

## Why it is hard to notice

No deauthentication, no flood, nothing any rate-based rule watches for.
What the operator observes is a client that has stopped working, with a
clean deauth table and no obvious cause.

## The passive signature, and its trap

A BAR whose Starting Sequence Number jumps forward further than traffic
could account for. The trap is that SSN is a 12-bit field that wraps: a
station transmitting steadily wraps roughly every 4096 frames, which on
a busy link is minutes. Computed as a plain subtraction, 4090 to 10
reads as a jump of 4080 rather than an advance of 16, and the detector
fires constantly on healthy connections — green in CI, useless on air.

sloth compares modulo 4096, and exposes bar_ssn_forward_distance() so
the arithmetic is tested directly rather than only through the detector.

## Scope note

State is per (receiver, transmitter, TID). Traffic classes have
independent sequence spaces, so sharing state across TIDs reads every
interleaved voice and best-effort BAR as a jump.
